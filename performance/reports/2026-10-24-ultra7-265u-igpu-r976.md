# r976 on the Core Ultra 7 265U, focused, fit and full zoom, against r964, 2026-10-24

**Rig:** Intel Core Ultra 7 265U, 14 threads, 62 GiB; the city draws on the integrated Arrow Lake-U GPU (i915), the only GPU in the machine; a 1920×1200 screen, the city alone on an i3 workspace at 1920×1118, X11, Arch Linux, kernel 7.2.11.
**Builds:** botropolis at r976 (`cc2cd0c`), built with `perf-pprof-build`, go 1.27.3; against r964, the build the 0.3.0 figures came from.
Since r964: item 162's slices 13 and 14 (the shore's texture fits 1024 a side; z1.5 replaces z2 and is drawn only from zoom 1.75), and the items that landed beside them.
**History on disk:** 309 transcripts, 3.5 GiB, 180 projects, 19 `claude` processes; at r964, 260 transcripts, 3.4 GiB, 162 projects.
**Runs:** method.md run 7, `perf-fitzoom`, once per build, sampled every second, on the real display with the focus, holding the landing lock; load 2.3 at the start and 3.8 at the end.

## Findings

1. **Full zoom meets the budget on the real history**: **481–484 MiB** own plus GPU settled (mean 481), against ≤ 512 MiB; r964 held 566–568.
   Own memory 239 → 196 MiB and GPU 327 → 285: the GPU drop is the 38 MiB slices 13 and 14 predicted (12 of shore texture, 26 of kit pages), within 4.
2. **Fit meets it too**: **459–467 MiB** (mean 462); r964 held 441–444.
   GPU went down 8 MiB (235 → 227), close to the shore's 12, and own memory went up 27 (207 → 234), which neither slice touches: z1 and the fit view are unchanged.
   The history grew by 49 transcripts and 18 projects between the two runs, so this is a lead for a second run or an A/B, not a regression on one run's evidence.
3. The city drew 6,840 frames in 230 s, 12.4 ms of drawing at the last report.

## What it does not show

One run of each build, a day apart, on a history that had grown: the full-zoom drop is far larger than run-to-run noise, and the fit rise is not.
The two builds were not run one after the other on the same afternoon, which is what a city A/B (run 4) would do.
The first run of r976 the same day was rejected: the monitor was off, the city was never focused and drew no frame, and `perf-fitzoom` now refuses to start in that state and fails a run that drew nothing.
