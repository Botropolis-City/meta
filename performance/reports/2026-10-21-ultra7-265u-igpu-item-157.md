# The daemon without its heap cap, against r871, 2026-10-21

**Rig:** Intel Core Ultra 7 265U, 14 threads, 62 GiB; integrated Arrow Lake-U GPU (not used: no city was opened); 1920×1200, i3 on X11, Arch Linux, kernel 7.2.
It is the machine of [the 0.2.2 report on the 265U](2026-10-19-ultra7-265u-igpu-0.2.2.md), and like it has far more RAM than the reference machine in [budgets.md](../budgets.md), so every figure is judged against the budgets.
**Builds:** r871 (`458454f`), main just before item 157, with the 16 MiB heap cap and a forced release after every rescan; r873 (`be5360c`), item 157's slice 1, with a 256 MiB limit and no per-rescan release; 0.1.7 (r409, `62c50ee`); each built with `go build ./cmd/botropolisd`, go 1.27.3.
**History on disk:** 204 transcripts, 3.2 GiB, 141 projects, 14 written in the last hour, 24 `claude` processes running, several item sessions among them.
**Runs:** daemon A/B (600 s at 1 s) and cold start (until idle, at 1 s), all three builds side by side, between 20:09 and 20:25 MDT.

## Findings

1. **The collector is no longer the daemon's work.** Settled, r871 made 1,104 collections a minute and r873 38; cold, 23,956 against 509 (A/B and cold start).
   The collector's share of all 14 cores, from gctrace, went from 2% (about 28% of one core, most of r871's 23.8% mean) to 0%.
2. **First launch costs a quarter of the CPU and a quarter of the peak**: 265 CPU-seconds and a peak of 394% of a core for r871, 73 CPU-seconds and 114% for r873, reading the same history in about 250 s each (cold start).
   The budget is ≤ 1 core in all, so 114% for one second-long sample is still just over it.
3. **Settled CPU fell by two thirds and is still over budget**: 23.8% mean and 140% at p95 for r871, 7.6% and 65% for r873, against ≤ 2% and ≤ 25% (A/B).
   Without the first minute, which both spent catching up, r873 averaged 4.7% and r871 14.2%; the busy minutes were minutes 3–5, when r873 ran 10–16%.
   What is left is the rescans' own work, not collection, and this item does not touch it.
4. **The memory it buys is small**: r873 settled at 26 MiB of `Private_Dirty` mean and peaked at 33 MiB, r871 at 20 and 23, against ≤ 64 MiB settled and ≤ 256 MiB at first launch (A/B and cold start).
5. **0.1.7 cannot be measured on this history**: it exits with `context deadline exceeded` about 10 s after starting, settled or cold, because it reads the whole home before it serves and gives up at its start-up timeout.
   Item 143 moved reading after start-up for that reason; the 0.1.7 figures in [budgets.md](../budgets.md) come from smaller histories.

## What it does not show

- **0.1.7, side by side.** It did not run long enough on this history to be compared; see finding 5.
- **Whether a 256 MiB limit ever binds.** The live heap never passed 11 MB here, so these runs show no cap rather than the new one; a history large enough to reach it was not available.
- **Where the remaining settled CPU goes.** No profile was taken; `perf-pprof-build` and `/debug/pprof/profile` would show it.
- **Reads.** r873 read about 6% more than r871 during the cold start (4,257 against 3,994 MiB), within what two daemons racing the same live sessions differ by; no budget for reads was judged here.
- **A reference machine.** Like every report so far, this is a 14-thread laptop, not the 4-core minimum.
