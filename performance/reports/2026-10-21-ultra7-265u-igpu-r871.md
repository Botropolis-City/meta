# r871 against 0.2.2 on the Core Ultra 7 265U, 2026-10-21

**Rig:** the same as [the 0.2.2 report](2026-10-19-ultra7-265u-igpu-0.2.2.md): Intel Core Ultra 7 265U, 14 threads, 62 GiB; the city draws on the **integrated Arrow Lake-U GPU** (i915), the only GPU in the machine; 1920×1200, i3 on X11, Arch Linux, kernel 7.2.11.
**Builds:** botropolis at r871 (`458454f`, unreleased; the roadmap manager's request called it r870, but `git rev-list --count` gives 871) and 0.2.2 (r745, `29b18c5`), both from source with `perf-pprof-build` plus `go build -tags devtools`, go 1.27.3.
Items 191–195 landed between them: the world's shapes as meshes and stamps, building looks cached, a still city composed once with only movers redrawn, and a gate on a frame's work.
**History on disk:** 198 transcripts, 3.2 GiB, 144 projects, 8 written in the last hour, about 25 `claude` processes running; larger than on 2026-10-19 (122, 2.4 GiB).
**Runs**, between 19:34 and 20:40 MDT, every city against a daemon of its own build on a **copy** of the state directory, so the real daemon's state was never touched, and every city's own state private (`XDG_STATE_HOME` and `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` in a scratch directory):

- **A**, the city on the real history for 600 s, hidden at 300 s (method.md, run 3), with heap profiles at 0–3 s and 60 s;
- **B**, r871 for 240 s with an idle `--script`, unfocused, with a CPU profile from 60 to 120 s;
- **F**, the same as A for 150 s with a CPU profile from 4 to 64 s, for each build;
- **C**, a recording on the real display through a script (settled 0–20 s, an arrow held 20–40 s, the wheel turned in and out 40–60 s, settled 60–72 s), on the `metropolis` demo and on the real history, for each build, with a CPU profile; and a zoom-first script on the real history for r871.

## Before reading the numbers

- **Focus decides whether a city draws, and the desk was in use.** Run A's window took the focus when it opened and drew 1,080 frames over its first 112 s; runs F did not get it and drew nothing.
  So the frame figures come from A and from recordings, which draw every frame whatever the focus.
- **A recording's frame figure is the drawing alone.** `BOTROPOLIS_FRAMETIME` times the wall clock around `Game.Draw`; a recording's PNG encoding runs on other goroutines and is not in it, though it competes for cores.
  A recording's process CPU and memory are **not** quoted: encoding dominates both.
- **The group-review panel was open in every run** (`firstRun`, item 87): the copied state has not reviewed its groups, so the real city would open with it too.
  It is in the frames and the heap.

## Findings, against [budgets.md](../budgets.md)

1. **The start-up burst is gone.** r871's Go heap peaks at 368–434 MB with 186–218 MB live, and its own memory + GPU in the first 10 s is **403–414 MiB** (runs A and F). Budget ≤ 1 GB: **met**.
   0.2.2 in the same session reached 3,111 MB of heap with 2,517 MB live.
2. **Settled, r871 holds 715–817 MiB of own memory + GPU** (run A, 60–300 s; run F, 70–150 s). Budget ≤ 512 MiB: **over by 200–300 MiB**.
   0.2.2 in the same session: **4,640 MiB**.
   GPU memory is 196–331 MiB of it (0.2.2: 1,265–1,669).
   At 60 s, 96 MB of the 202 MB heap is kit-page pixels still held after upload (`kits.uploadNext` → Ebitengine's `graphics.bytesPool`) and 51 MB is text shaping for the group-review panel (`firstRun.draw` → `faces.Measure` → HarfBuzz).
3. **Drawing a frame of the real history costs 17–48 ms in r871**: 17–48 while focused (run A), 18–30 settled, **41–54 panning** and **25–31 zooming** (recorded). Budget ≤ 10 ms: **over by 2–5×**.
   On `metropolis` it is **6–13 ms settled, 9–15 panning, 11–13 zooming**, against 20–172 ms for 0.2.2 under the same script: about six times cheaper, and at the budget on the demo.
   On the real history 0.2.2 cannot be measured this way at all: it recorded 62 frames, 2 s of its clock, in 150 s.
4. **A focused r871 city uses 83% of a core** while drawing at about ten frames a second (run A, 10–112 s). Budget ≤ 10% of a core: **over by 8×**.
5. **A city that is not drawing still works on every update from the daemon.** Unfocused it used 5.9–8.3% of a core (runs B and F), and hidden 7.9%, with seconds at 20–34% (run A, 330–600 s). Budget hidden ≤ 1%: **over by 8×**.
   The CPU profile of an unfocused city (60 s) puts **76% of it in `Scene.SetSnapshot` → `city.build`**: the whole city is laid out again for every snapshot, and within that `group.Order` (38%; regular expressions over every site's words, and one compiled per site and version in `tagged`) and `plan.Make` → `plantGround` (29%).
   Nothing on the roadmap covers it: items 191–195 are about drawing.
6. **A hidden city keeps 462–464 MiB of its own memory and 315 MiB of GPU** 30 s and 300 s after hiding (run A). Budget ≤ 256 MiB own within 30 s: **over by about 200 MiB**, and nothing is let go on hiding.

## What it does not show

- **0.2.2's frame time on the real history**, which it could not record; and **r871's focused CPU** came from one window that happened to keep the focus for 112 s, so it is one run.
- **Panning and zooming without recording**: they need input, and only a recording plays a script, so their cost is the drawing figure alone, not the process's CPU.
- **The daemon**, which was not re-measured: items 157–160 are about it.
- **macOS**, and **anything longer than ten minutes**, as before (item 164).

## Reproduce

From the meta repository's root, `T` a scratch directory:

```sh
performance/tools/perf-pprof-build 458454f $T/r871 && (cd $T/r871/src && go build -tags devtools -o $T/r871/botropolis-dev ./cmd/botropolis)
performance/tools/perf-pprof-build v0.2.2 $T/v022 && (cd $T/v022/src && go build -tags devtools -o $T/v022/botropolis-dev ./cmd/botropolis)
(cd $T/r871/src && go build -o $T/botropolis-demo ./cmd/botropolis-demo)
$T/botropolis-demo stage --corpus botropolis/demo/corpus --scenario botropolis/demo/scenarios/metropolis.yaml --home $T/home
# a daemon of each build on a copy of ~/.local/state/botropolis, its own socket and a stub claude, then:
PERF_PPROF=127.0.0.1:6073 performance/tools/perf-city --bin $T/r871/botropolis-dev --seconds 600 --hide-after 300 -- --socket <that socket>
# recordings, settled 0-20 s, an arrow held 20-40 s, the wheel 40-60 s:
$T/r871/botropolis-dev city --home $T/home --socket /nonexistent/b.sock --window 1600x900 --script panzoom.json --record <dir> --seconds 72 --fps 30
```
