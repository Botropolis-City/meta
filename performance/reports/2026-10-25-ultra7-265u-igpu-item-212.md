# The dry pass after item 212's first three slices, 2026-10-25

[Item 212](../../roadmap/212-every-frame-re-records-every-drawable.md)'s first checkpoint: what recording the frame costs at r1041 against r954, at fit and at full zoom, on the real display.
The item closes when the dry pass is under 2% of a core settled at both.

**Rig:** Intel Core Ultra 7 265U, 14 threads, 62 GiB; its integrated GPU drawing; 1920×1200, i3 on X11, Arch Linux, kernel 7.2.
It is the machine of [the item 157 report](2026-10-21-ultra7-265u-igpu-item-157.md).
**Builds:** r954 (`9e6b264`) and r1041 (`ee7891d`, slices 1–3), each from `perf-pprof-build`.
**History on disk:** today's, the daemon on a copy of the state directory with a stub `claude`.
**Runs:** `perf-fitzoom --no-wait`, one per build, on Aria's display with her leave: focused, at fit to 100 s, then 40 wheel notches in, settled at full zoom to 220 s, with CPU profiles over 30–90 s and 150–210 s.
The machine was not quiet, and the run did not wait for it: r954 ran from load 1.3 to 86 as three item sessions started, and r1041, which waited 32 minutes on the landing lock, ran from 5 to 3.4 with landings held.

## Findings

1. **`note` itself fell by more than half and is still three to four times the target**: 14.2% → 5.9% of a core at fit and 16.5% → 7.3% at full zoom.
2. **The recording moved more than it shrank.** The slices added `noteKeyed`, with `keep` and `noteFresh` under it, `noteBuilding` and `noteAgedLots`; at full zoom `noteKeyed` is 9.9% of a core and `noteBuilding` 7.9%, and `agedLots`, gone by name, is `noteAgedLots` at 3.3%.
   Per frame, the city's whole `Draw` rose from 15.0 to 17.0 ms at full zoom and from 12.2 to 18.4 ms at fit.
3. **Keeping last frame's record costs about what recording afresh did**: `keep` and `noteFresh` take 2.83 and 2.22 s of a 60 s profile at full zoom.
4. **A target named after one function can be met by moving work**, so item 212's measure should be the dry pass inclusive: every `note*` function and the record keeping under them.
