# The 2,000-session daemon under its budget, for item 213, 2026-10-26

[Item 213](../../roadmap/213-the-2000-session-daemon-is-over-its-cpu-budget.md)'s check before it closes: a settled daemon on the synthetic home plus 2,000 small sessions, one session writing a reply every 200 ms and a prompt every 5 s, judged on the quieter of two 30-second windows against 2% of a core.

**Rig:** Intel Core Ultra 7 265U, 14 threads, 62 GiB; the daemon only, on a synthetic home, no display; Arch Linux, kernel 7.2.
It is the machine of [the item 157 report](2026-10-21-ultra7-265u-igpu-item-157.md).
**Builds:** before, r1075 (`071a6ae`), the last revision before item 213's slices; after, r1089 (`e458c9f`), its final head, slices 1–5; each in a detached worktree at its commit, with its own test file, whose stream and home are the same in both.
**Runs:** `make test-performance RUN='^TestDaemonWithTwoThousandSessions$'`, five pairs, after then before, each started on a quiet machine (load under 3 over one minute and 4 over five), recorded.
The rule, agreed with the roadmap manager: every after-run under 2%, and the after-runs' worst below the before-runs' best.

## Findings

1. **The daemon is under its budget in every run**: the after-runs' gated share is 1.65–1.79% of a core against 2%, and every run exited 0, the after build's new rescan-cost subtest included.
2. **It is about a percentage point of a core cheaper, 37% less**: the after-runs' worst, 1.79%, is below the before-runs' best, 2.54%; counted by its CPU ticks, 1.46–1.69% against 2.19–2.56%.
3. **The margin is in the quieter window**: the after build's other window read 1.99% and 1.96% in two pairs, so a gate on either window would sit at the edge.

**The gate holds**: every run is under 1% in both unfocused cases, the worst 0.92% at 15 s and 0.84% at 65 s, and every run exited 0.
The 15-second case has the thinner margin, 0.08 of a percentage point at its worst, so it is the first to watch if the city's unfocused work grows.

## What it does not show

- **The live home.** A synthetic home of 2,000 small sessions; a real history's daemon is measured by `perf-daemon-ab`.
- **Another desk.** One laptop, 14 threads; the 4-core reference machine was not measured.

## Reproduce

```sh
make -C <worktree at 071a6ae> test-performance RUN='^TestDaemonWithTwoThousandSessions$'
```

Alternate the two five times, each from load under 3 over one minute and under 4 over five.
