# r905 on the Core Ultra 7 265U, focused, against r871, 2026-10-22

**Rig:** the same as [the r871 report](2026-10-21-ultra7-265u-igpu-r871.md): Intel Core Ultra 7 265U, 14 threads, 62 GiB; the city draws on the **integrated Arrow Lake-U GPU** (i915), the only GPU in the machine; 1920×1200, i3 on X11, Arch Linux, kernel 7.2.11.
**Build:** botropolis at r905 (`e6bde10`), from source with `perf-pprof-build` plus `go build -tags devtools`, go 1.27.3.
Since r871: items 159, 162, 196, 197 and 200 (kit pixels freed after upload and one zoom level resident, the city laid out once per change, a hidden city unsubscribed, cheap daemon rescans).
**History on disk:** 217 transcripts, 3.2 GiB, 147 projects, about 15 `claude` processes running.
**Runs:** one timeline per home, 720 s, sampled at 1 s, on the real display, **with the focus**: Aria gave the screen to these runs, and the landing lock was held throughout so no item session landed under them.

| seconds | phase |
| --- | --- |
| 0–10 | start-up |
| 10–90 | settled at fit, focused; CPU profile 30–90 s |
| 90–120 | panning: an arrow held for 4.8 s, six times |
| 120–150 | zooming: the wheel 6 notches in and out, five times |
| 150–180 | the wheel 40 notches in, to full zoom |
| 180–270 | settled at full zoom, focused |
| 270–300 | the wheel 40 notches out |
| 300–420 | the focus given back to the window that had it: visible and unfocused; CPU profile 330–390 s |
| 420–720 | hidden in i3's scratchpad; CPU profile 450–510 s |

- **The real history**: a daemon of the same build on a **copy** of the state directory, its own socket and a stub `claude`; `~/.claude` read only; the city's own state and config private. The real daemon and its state were not touched.
- **`metropolis`**: the demo staged by `botropolis-demo stage`, no daemon.

Input came through XTEST from the system's `libXtst`, called with Python's `ctypes`: a real pointer, wheel and keyboard on a focused window, so the process's CPU is quotable, unlike a recording's.
The window was focused again every ten seconds while focused phases ran.
The wheel phases certainly registered: the static layer was reused 75, 0 and 51 times in 120 frames while zooming, against 113–120 otherwise.
**The panning phase is not certain**: reuse stayed at 113–116 of 120 and the cost matched settled, so either a pan offsets the composed layer cheaply or the held arrows did not move the camera.

The group-review panel (`firstRun`, item 87) was open in every run, as it would be for Aria: the state directory has not reviewed its groups.
The journal was drawn too.

## Findings, against [budgets.md](../budgets.md)

1. **Settled memory at fit meets the budget on the real history**: **398–400 MiB** of own memory and GPU together (151 MiB `Private_Dirty`, 247 MiB GPU), against ≤ 512 MiB; r871 held 715–817.
2. **At full zoom it does not**: **618–620 MiB** (246 + 372), over by about 110 MiB. On `metropolis`, full zoom holds 479–481 MiB and meets it.
3. **A frame at fit draws in 8.7 ms on the real history** (median of 19 reports, 8.2–11.9), against 17–48 at r871, and in 5.5 ms on `metropolis`. Budget ≤ 10 ms: **met at fit**.
   **At full zoom it is 18.9 ms** (18.2–23.6): **not met**, about twice the budget. Zooming costs 9.2 ms (up to 21.7 while the zoom level changes); panning 8.6.
4. **A focused, settled city still uses 61% of a core on the real history and 36% on `metropolis`**; 84% at full zoom. Budget ≤ 10% of a core: **not met, by 6× (real) and 3.6× (demo)**.
   The frame-time figure is wall clock around `Game.Draw`; the CPU profile puts 25.6 s of CPU in `Draw` over 60 s at about 28.5 frames a second, **about 15 ms of CPU a frame**, because `Draw` runs on more than one thread.
   Of `Draw`: the world (`drawIso`) is 45%, and half of that is **text drawn every frame** — the map's notes and labels through `note` → `text` → `text/v2.Draw` (25% of `Draw`), and `agedLots` (12%); overlays are 31%: the journal panel 16%, the group-review panel 9%.
5. **A city that is not drawing no longer costs CPU**: unfocused and visible, **1.4% of a core** mean on the real history (p95 2.0%, one second at 62%) and 2.3% on `metropolis`; the sampled profile is 0.5% of a core, a third of it laying out a snapshot and a third decoding updates.
   This is item 201's baseline; r871 used 5.9–8.3%.
6. **A hidden city meets both its budgets**: **0.8% of a core**, and **162 MiB** of own memory 30 s after hiding and 193 MiB at 300 s, against ≤ 1% and ≤ 256 MiB; r871 kept 464 MiB and 7.9%.
   Own memory rose 31 MiB over the five hidden minutes on the real history, while the Go heap stayed at 90–96 MB; it is a lead for item 164's soak, not a leak on ten minutes' evidence.
   The hidden heap still holds 31 MB of kit-page bytes (`graphics.bytesPool`) from full zoom and 23 MB of the draw-command queue.
7. **The start-up peak is 420 MiB** own + GPU on the real history and 395 on `metropolis` (≤ 1 GB), with a Go heap peak of 242 MB at full zoom and 123 MB live at most.

## Reproduce

From the meta repository's root, `T` a scratch directory, holding the landing lock:

```sh
flock /tmp/botropolis-land.lock sleep 14400 &
performance/tools/perf-pprof-build e6bde10 $T/r905 && (cd $T/r905/src && go build -tags devtools -o $T/r905/botropolis-dev ./cmd/botropolis)
# a daemon of the build on a copy of ~/.local/state/botropolis, its own socket, a stub claude; then
PERF_PPROF=127.0.0.1:6075 performance/tools/perf-city --bin $T/r905/botropolis-dev --seconds 720 --interval 1 -- --socket <that socket>
# while it runs: focus the window with i3-msg "[id=<window>] focus", and drive it through XTEST
# (XTestFakeMotionEvent, XTestFakeButtonEvent with buttons 4 and 5, XTestFakeKeyEvent) on the timeline above
```
