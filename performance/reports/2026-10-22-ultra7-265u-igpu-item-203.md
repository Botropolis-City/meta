# First launch after item 203, against r910, 2026-10-22

**Rig:** Intel Core Ultra 7 265U, 14 threads, 62 GiB; integrated Arrow Lake-U GPU (not used: no city was opened); 1920×1200, i3 on X11, Arch Linux, kernel 7.2.
It is the machine of [the item 159 report](2026-10-22-ultra7-265u-igpu-item-159.md), and every figure is judged against the *First launch* budgets in [budgets.md](../budgets.md).
**Builds:** r910 (`a085aed`), main just before item 203; r911 (`846d4b5`), item 203's slice 1; each built with `go build ./cmd/botropolisd` from `git archive`, go 1.27.3.
**History on disk:** 195 transcripts, 3,314 MiB; the 26 written in the last day hold 702 MiB, most of it in two sessions.
**Run:** cold start, until both had been idle for a minute, at 1 s, both builds side by side, holding `/tmp/botropolis-land.lock` so no landing's test suite shared the cores.
Each daemon had an empty state directory, socket, runtime and config directory of its own and a stub `claude`; `~/.claude` was only read.

## Findings

1. **No sample over one core.** r911's highest 1 s sample was 84%, its first; from 2 s on its paced passes held 50–57%.
   r910 ran its first load at 102–115% for its first 5 s, 5 samples over the budget, reading 184–221 MiB/s.
2. **The first load ends within about a second**, against about 5 s for r910 today (9 s in item 159's run, when the last day held more).
   This is read from the samples, not timed from a snapshot: r911's CPU falls to the paced pass's half a core by its second sample, and r910's stays over a core until its first load ends.
   The suite's `TestFirstLaunchLoad` times it in bytes: 52 MiB before the first snapshot, against 434 MiB without the item, on a home modelled on this one.
3. **Nothing is read twice for it**: 4,107 MiB in all against r910's 4,099 MiB, and 35.9 CPU-seconds against 35.1, both idle at 125 s.
   The long reads moved after the map, behind the same pacer, and were not added to.
4. **Memory is unchanged and far under budget**: `Private_Dirty` peaked at 41 MiB against r910's 40, against ≤ 256 MiB; 249 collections against 280.

## What it does not show

- **The city.** No window was opened; the first map's time is inferred from the daemon's samples, and the city's own side is gated by `TestFirstLaunchCity`.
- **A warm start's catalogue.** The item's second idea, serving stopped sessions from the statistics store instead of their windows, would save only those windows: on this home they are part of a first load that now ends inside a second, so it was not built (item 203's deviations).
- **A reference machine.** Like every report so far, this is a 14-thread laptop, not the 4-core minimum.
